## Identity Kit

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body. Nothing decorative — I want it to read as technical and clean, not "designed."

**Colors:**
- Navy `#1E3A8A` — main color, headings and logo
- Teal `#14B8A6` — the only accent, used just for links and buttons
- Near-black `#111827` — body text
- Near-white `#FAFAFA` — background

**Logo:** a simple "OK" monogram in Space Grotesk Bold — navy on white for the main logo, or a navy square with a teal "OK" for the favicon.

**Style note I'll paste back into every build session:**
"Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body. Navy anchors the brand, teal is the only accent and it's reserved for links/CTAs so the project screenshots stay the loudest thing on the page. Generous whitespace, max 2 fonts, max 4 colors."
